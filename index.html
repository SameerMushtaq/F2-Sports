Quick FAQs & Bat Care

Willow Grade Differences?
Grade A features 8-11 straight grains with minimal blemishes for match play. Grade B has 6-8 grains offering power and durability at a competitive price.
How to pay via UPI / QR?
Add to cart, proceed to checkout, scan verified UPI QR code, and upload your payment screenshot.
Do bats come pre-knocked and oiled?
Yes, all our willow bats undergo initial machine knocking and linseed oil seasoning. We recommend light net sessions with old leather balls before competitive match play.
What is the expected delivery timeframe?
We offer pan-India express dispatch. Deliveries generally arrive in 3 to 5 business days with live tracking provided upon shipment.
