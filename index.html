Quick FAQs & Bat Care

1. Willow Grade Differences?
Grade A features 8-11 straight grains with minimal blemishes for match play. Grade B has 6-8 grains offering power and durability.

Yes, all our willow bats undergo initial machine knocking and linseed oil seasoning before dispatch.

We offer pan-India express dispatch. Deliveries generally arrive in 3 to 5 business days with live tracking provided.
